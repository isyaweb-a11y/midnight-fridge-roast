One line roasting a smart fridge that refuses to open after midnight — under 200 characters, judged head to head until the funniest line is left standing.

## The entry

> My smart fridge locks itself at midnight — apparently the leftovers needed a safe word.

87 characters, one line. The roast flips the power dynamic: the fridge isn't protecting the food from spoiling, it's protecting the food from you — and the leftovers have apparently negotiated consent terms.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3, standard library only) validates the entry — one line, non-blank, at most 200 characters — writes it to `/output/entry.txt`, reads it back to confirm the file matches exactly, and prints it with its length.

Run it:

```bash
python3 main.py
```
